## The game
Carry-and-build: fill your bag in the Blocks pit (stand in the yellow zone), ride the hoist (or walk the stairs) to the roof of the shared skyscraper and place blocks within Build Range of the build front. Every floor that fills grows the tower; when the last floor is done the building gets its crown, stands for a moment, sinks into the sand and a bigger one in a new shape starts (`BUILDINGS`). The elevator masts, stair tower and cranes always match the building's current height. Strength (bench press) sets bag capacity and bulks up the avatar; Speed (treadmill) sets walk speed. Gym pads unlock by buildings finished (premium pads with cash). Upgrades (`UPGRADES`, cash only, each row shows "now » next" via its `fmt`): Block Grab, Build Speed, Build Range, Elevator Speed, plus Golden Touch / Fortune Luck (every placed block has a Fortune Luck % chance of a lucky drop worth Golden Touch coins x `LUCKY.coinValue` x cash multiplier, `rollLucky`, paid in main.js `luckyDrop`), Coin Combo (placing without a break fills a combo, `COMBO`, that multiplies placed-block cash up to the upgrade's value; `tickCombo`/`comboMul`, shown under the HUD cash), Wide Trowel (`trowelMul`: x blocks per bag block, fractions carry over via `placeCarry`) and Lightning Hands (extra Build Speed multiplier). 30 ranks by total blocks placed (`RANKS`, +3% cash each). Daily & Playtime Rewards + Free Reward (below), rotating world events, day/night cycle, leaderboard wall with podium statues. Robot Gear hall (`ROBOT_GEAR`, west of the leaderboard): wearable robot gear bought with cash (Jet Boots $25K to Titan Armor $150B), one piece worn at a time; each multiplies blocks placed per bag block and Build Speed and adds Walk Speed (HUD units) and capacity. Full suits (`size`) are a 2x giant robot the unscaled player pilots; add-on gear (boots, gauntlets, arms) is built ~2x around the limbs. Jet Boots / Black Mech Suit / Titan Armor fly (`fly`, hold Jump). Transformers build their vehicle piece by piece out of the robot on G and drive on sprung suspension (the Jet Transformer flies); Titan Armor's G calls a dropship. Big drops (the tower roof): in Robot Gear you free-fall and land on the leg shock absorbers in a superhero landing (`impact.js`, `ROBOT.impact`: landing faster than `minVy` -> knee-down crouch via the animator's `impact` activity, walk slowed while it holds, cracked-ground decal, rubble via `fx.flyBlock`, shock rings, steam from the legs, `shakeCamera` in camera.js, sounds `impactLand` / `impactRise`); without gear a parachute opens: `parachute.js` (`ROBOT.chute`, red/white canopy `color`/`glow`; opens once the ground is near, earlier the faster you fall; ram-air canopy that unfurls cell by cell, glides forward, banks into turns, flares in the last metres and collapses where you land; free-fall air streaks, deploy snap ring, tip vortex trails, a glow-coloured smoke trail; sounds `chuteDeploy` / `chuteFlare` / `chuteLand` + the `canopy` / `rush` loops in `audio/robot.js`; `player.chute` brakes the fall in `player.js`, `groundBelow()` measures the drop; the animator's `chute` activity is the harness pose). Spider Arms (`carry`, `climb`, legs 1.5x the Robotic Arms'): the player rides high on eight legs that do the walking (their own legs hang still; the body lifts until the lowest claw rests on the ground) and they climb the tower (walk into a wall to latch on, forward / back climbs, strafe along it, Jump lets go, the top hauls you onto the roof; `ROBOT.climb`, `player.climb`). Outside the hall: a LIMITED Spider Arms pad (`spot: 'limited'`, stock + countdown from `stats.limitedStatus`, restocks every window) and a showcase pad (`LAYOUT.robot.showcase`). Particles + synthesised sounds for every action.

**Economy curve (easy start, hard top; tuned with a Monte Carlo of the whole economy):** upgrade prices run along `ladder(first, last, max)` in config.js (cheap early levels, steep near the top; ~$7T to max all nine), gear goes Jet Boots $25K -> Titan Armor $150B, eggs $2K / $100K / $500M / $500B, the premium gym pads ($5T / $50T) are post-max goals, capacity grows with Strength^0.6, a lucky coin is worth 0.5, and **pets boost cash only, never gym gains** (cash -> eggs -> pets -> Strength -> cash used to max the game in ~1 h). Result (since floors are sized by time, below): ~30 purchases in the first 15 minutes, every upgrade maxed after ~52 h of solo play (~62 h building in a room of 5, `CROWD=5`: buildings, which unlock gym pads and give spins, come slower in a crowd). Re-run the sim (`[CROWD=n] node tools/economySim.mjs [runs] [casual|average|grinder]`, it reads src/config.js) before changing prices or multipliers. What keeps a player going between purchases: a floor every ~3.5 min alone, ~4.75-6 min in a crowd (sized by time, below) with an income-scaled floor bonus, Goals, buildings, daily / playtime / offline rewards.

**Currency rule (root CLAUDE.md): nothing is ever bought with Gems/Robux.** Every shop item, pass, premium gym pad and instant-block boost costs in-game cash (`SHOP`, `GYM.tiers[].price`).

Three.js (vanilla, no React) + Vite + Rapier (`@dimforge/rapier3d-compat`, wasm inlined; `main.js` awaits its init at top level, hence `build.target: 'es2022'`). Everything is procedural (canvas textures); the only imported model is the Bloxity base rig `public/avatars/player.glb`. Font: Fredoka (loaded in `index.html`).

Deploy: `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` uploads `dist/` to Bloxity Hosting (main = prod, dev = dev channel) for game ID/slug `build-the-skyscraper`; only the `LEGION_DEPLOY_TOKEN` secret is needed.

## Files (`src/`)
- `main.js`: boot (Bloxity SDK -> renderer -> Rapier -> load save -> desert, site, pit, tower, elevator, stairs, cranes, fx, gym, leaderboard -> player body -> avatar -> `warmup.full()` behind the loading screen) and the per-frame game loop in `frame()` (wrapped in try/catch so an error never stops the loop): pit collecting, roof building, gym training, tower/rank/event reactions, guide arrow, HUD snapshot, autosave. `__game` (dev only) adds `state`, `tower`, `elevator`, `gym`, `buildings(n)` (sets buildings finished and opens gym pads), `give({cash, strength, speed})`, `blocks(n)`, `view([x,y,z],[tx,ty,tz])` (free camera; `view(null)` releases it), `spins(n)`, `pet(id, tier)`, `reset()` (wipes the save).
- **Network**: `net.js` the Colyseus link. Joins `world` with `{ token | devId (dev only), username, pfp, pose, loadout, avatar }` (built games reach the server only through the Bloxity matchmaker, `npm run dev` uses `VITE_SERVER_URL_DEV` or `ws://localhost:2567`; a `?roomId=` launch joins that room first). `initNet()` starts it, `await whenLoaded()` (main.js, before the tower is built) waits for the server's `progress` (hydrates `state`, replacing the browser save) / `noProgress` (keeps the browser save for a guest or a new account, unless it belongs to another account) / `serverError` (saving off), or gives up after `NET.loadTimeout`. `saveNow(force)` writes localStorage and, once loaded, `saveProgress` (change-only, every `NET.saveEvery` s). A guest signing in (or out) after joining flushes the old identity's save and reloads into the same room (`?roomId=`). Presence goes up from `setPresenceSources` (pose ~`NET.poseHz`/s, `{rank, strength, gear}`, the avatar as equipped IDS only), `roster()` / `mySessionId()` / `myUid()` / `sendEmote(id)` are what the other-player code uses, the server's `leaderboard` goes to `leaderboard.js` `setRemote` (top 10 per board merged with the bots and you).
- **Shared tower** (one per room, `ROOM_MAX_CLIENTS` = 50, owned by the server's `src/tower.ts`; wire contract in the backend CLAUDE.md): everyone in the room builds and sees the SAME building. `net.towerState()` is the room's `{ n, floors, blocks, need, placed, phase, phaseAt, seeded }` (null while offline / not seeded: then the tower plays locally exactly as before; `need` = the floor's size, `placed` = blocks on this building), `net.serverNow()` the server clock (offset from the `serverTime` of `progress`/`noProgress`) that ages `phaseAt`, `net.sendBuild(n)` batches the blocks you place into `build { blocks, trip }` (`trip` = `stats.tripSize()`, blocks one full bag of yours places, from `net.setTripSource`) (<= every `NET.buildEvery` s, flushed when the tab hides), `net.onBuilt(fn)` gets the server's `built { n, builders }`. main.js builds the tower from `net.towerState() ?? state.tower` (whenLoaded waits for the seed), calls `tower.setReporter(net.sendBuild)`, `tower.setBuilders` from `onBuilt` (your own entry becomes id `'me'` for the finale) and `tower.sync(net.towerState(), net.serverNow())` every frame. `tower.js` `sync(ts, now)`: while shared (`tower.shared`) the displayed progress and phase ONLY follow the server (the local celebrate -> sinking timer and the next-building rise are off): more floors/blocks advance through the normal `completeFloor` visuals and events (`floor`, `complete`, `height`), a new `n` or a state behind ours (another room) rebuilds through `setBuilding`, `celebrate` / `sinking` age from `phaseAt` (`openLeft = INSIDE.time - age`, the sink starts `age` s in, a late joiner sees the open lobby / the sinking building at the right moment). `addBlocks(n, who)` fills AT MOST the floor in progress per call (local and shared; the server caps each `build` the same way; `roomLeft()` = what still fits), so no placement or instant-block boost (main.js `buyBoost`: places min(blocks, roomLeft, what is left of `SHOP.boostShare` = 25% of the floor for boosts) and charges only for those) can finish a building in one go. In shared mode `addBlocks(n, who)` is optimistic accounting only: it returns what still fits per the server state minus blocks reported but not yet seen (so bag / cash code is unchanged), reports them and leaves the displayed progress to the server (~100 ms later). Losing the connection (`towerState()` null) hands the tower back to the local timers from where it is; the rejoin makes the server win again. Rewards stay per player (`helpedFloor` / `helpedBuilding`) and are sized to the player AND to how long the work took, so a crowd's longer floors never pay less per minute: the floor bonus is floor x `TOWER.floorBonus` x cash multiplier + `TOWER.floorIncome` s of `stats.recentIncome()` per minute the floor took for you (`floorStartedAt`, 1-8 min), the building bonus ((n + 1) x `buildingBonus` x cash multiplier + `buildingIncome` s of it x (the building's average floor minutes / `soloMins`, 1 to maxMins/soloMins)) x the building's rarity bonus. `recentIncome()` = building earnings (placed blocks + lucky drops, `noteEarned`, never rewards) averaged over `TOWER.incomeTau` s (`tickIncome` every frame), never below half of what a bag a minute would pay. There are no bots: only real players add blocks. Remote players on the finale's podium have no staged body yet (props and card only).

## Mobile & responsive (required for every change)
The game must play on phones and tablets (portrait and landscape) as well as desktop. Treat a feature as unfinished until it works on touch and on a small screen.
- **Input**: every action needs a touch path, not just a key. Use Pointer Events (`pointerdown/move/up`, check `e.pointerType`), never mouse-only events. Layout (implemented in `src/touch.js`): left-thumb virtual joystick -> `inputState.move`, right-side drag -> `inputState.look`, on-screen buttons for jump/sprint/mode/etc., pinch -> `inputState.zoom`. Everything still feeds `inputState` in `src/input.js` so game code stays input-agnostic. Pointer lock does not exist on touch; never rely on it.
- **Detection**: decide touch UI with `matchMedia('(pointer: coarse)')` / `navigator.maxTouchPoints`, not the user agent. Show touch controls only on coarse pointers; a device can have both.
- **Layout/UI**: HUD and menus use relative units (`%`, `vw/vh`, `dvh`, `clamp()`) and flexbox/grid, no fixed pixel layouts. Tap targets >= 44px. Respect notches with `env(safe-area-inset-*)` (viewport already has `viewport-fit=cover`). Text readable at 360px width. No hover-only interactions. Add `touch-action: none` on the canvas and game controls so the browser does not scroll/zoom.
- **Resize/orientation**: anything sized to the screen (camera aspect, renderer, HUD) must react to `resize` (covers orientation changes). Use `visualViewport` where the on-screen keyboard or browser bars matter.
- **Performance**: mobile GPUs are weak. Respect the `graphics_quality` presets in `src/config.js` (cap DPR, shadow map size, draw distance); default to a lower preset on coarse-pointer devices. Avoid per-frame allocations; keep draw calls and texture sizes low.
- **Testing**: check every UI/input change at a phone viewport (e.g. 375x812 and landscape 812x375) with touch emulation, plus desktop, before calling it done.
